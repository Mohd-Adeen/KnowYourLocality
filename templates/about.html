About KnowYourLocality

A civic intelligence platform transforming open government statistics into transparent, population-normalized safety insights for citizens, travelers, and policymakers.

Data Powered by NCRB & Census of India

Our Mission & Approach

Raw crime figures can be misleading. A metropolis of 10 million people will naturally report higher aggregate crime numbers than a rural district of 50,000, yet its per-person risk may actually be significantly lower.

KnowYourLocality eliminates this bias by cross-referencing official annual incident records from the National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB) with Census of India population benchmarks to deliver statistically sound, per-capita crime rates (per 100,000 citizens) and a transparent 0–100 Safety Score.

Per-Capita Accurate
Crimes normalized per 100,000 residents based on official census data.
Time-Series Forecasts
Statistical ARIMA models projecting 3-year trend trajectories.
Official Data Provenance
  • Source Authority: National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB), Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India.
  • Data Platform: Open Government Data (OGD) Platform India (data.gov.in).
  • Population Benchmark: Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India (2011 Census).
  • License: Open Government Data License - India (OGDL).

How the Safety Score is Calculated

1. Data De-duplication
Summary rows and administrative totals are excluded to prevent double-counting.
2. Census Normalization
Crimes are adjusted against local population to derive annual rate per 100k.
3. Domain Scoring
Sub-scores computed for Violent, Women's Safety, Property, and Financial offenses.
4. Predictive Trend
Auto-Regressive Integrated Moving Average (ARIMA) models forecast trajectory.
