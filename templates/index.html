Emergency & Safety

National Emergency Helplines

Government of India toll-free services — available 24 × 7

100
Police Emergency

Immediate police response for crimes in progress, threats to life, and law-and-order incidents. Available 24 × 7 across all Indian states.

Call 100
101
Fire Brigade

Fire emergencies, building collapses, gas leaks, and rescue operations. Operated by state fire departments with rapid deployment.

Call 101
102
Ambulance Services

Medical emergencies, road accidents, and maternal transport. Free ambulance service under the National Health Mission (NHM).

Call 102
112
National Emergency

Single-point national emergency number integrating Police, Fire & Medical. Works even without a SIM card on any mobile phone. First call in any crisis.

Call 112
1091 / 181
Women & Girls Helpline

Immediate support for women facing harassment, domestic violence, or distress. 181 provides counselling and shelter referrals, operated by state governments.

Call 1091 Call 181
1098
Childline India

24-hour emergency support for children in distress — abuse, trafficking, missing children. Managed by Ministry of Women & Child Development.

Call 1098
1930
Cyber Crime Helpline

Report online fraud, financial scams, cyber stalking & digital crimes. Also report at cybercrime.gov.in — managed by Ministry of Home Affairs.

Call 1930

About This Platform

Crime data is sourced from NCRB (National Crime Records Bureau) annual reports (official district reports 2014–2024 across all 9 crime domains). Safety scores use Census of India 2011 benchmarks. For informational & research purposes only. Does not constitute legal advice.
