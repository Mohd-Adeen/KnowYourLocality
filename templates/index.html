About Us

We are a team of final-year Computer Engineering students passionate about using technology to address real-world issues. As part of our academic project, we are developing a website focused on analyzing and understanding crime patterns across India. Our platform combines data analytics, machine learning, and visualization tools to provide users with valuable insights into crime trends, hotspots, and safety patterns. By making crime data more accessible and understandable, we aim to empower citizens, law enforcement, and policymakers to make informed decisions and take proactive steps towards a safer India. Through innovation and collaboration, we strive to contribute to a safer, more secure society.
